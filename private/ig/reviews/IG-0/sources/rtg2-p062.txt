Redesign Notes: This was the first scenario I designed; I did the research way back in December, 1998! The original scenario had 2 variants: “Lee’s Retreat” 
and “The Battle Continues” . In this re-design the ‘Lee’s Retreat” variant has been eliminated. It was a bit complicated and would not convert well from 
the original recovery rules to the Standard Rules. “The Battle Continues” on the other hand has been a popular slugfest played repeatedly at the W orld 
Boardgaming Championships. The only changes to “The Battle Continues” from the original publication are converting the setup to the Standard Rules and 
incorporating the typical Union 2 bid into the victory conditions to re-balance the scenario.
