One surprising outcome of this redesign is all the new scenarios I was able 
to devise for a campaign with only one battle, and yet with only two of them 
being what-if.  The new scenarios are: Stuart Rides North, The Pipe Creek

Fight.  Play-testing all this new and redesigned material was done by a core 
team of regular testers – please see the credits below for all the members of 
this group.  Special thanks are in order to Steve Likevich, Ian Mackay, and 
Niall T aylor.  These three experts replayed scenarios endlessly with me and 
each other to get them balanced; and they helped with some of the research
