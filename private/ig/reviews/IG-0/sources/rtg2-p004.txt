NOTES: This scenario simulates Lee’s desperate attempt to hold back 
McClellan’s Army of the Potomac at the South Mountain passes while his 
scattered Army of Northern Virginia reunited west of the mountains.
MAP: Use only the west map.
GAME LENGTH: 1 turn, September 14, 1862.
SPECIAL RULES:
1. T urn 1: The Union player automatically wins the initiative die roll in 
the first Action Phase of the game.  Afterwards, initiative is determined 
normally.
2. Union Movement Restrictions: Union units may not enter or retreat 
into any hex adjacent to Burkittsville (W3117) or Knoxville (W3020).
