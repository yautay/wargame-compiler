Unless otherwise specified all corps assets (non-divisional artillery units followed by the baggage train) arrive at the end of the column. 6.4, 19.23 Combat Strength: The total manpower of a Combat Unit quantified in Strength Points (SPs). Only Combat Units may attack enemy Combat Units.  Combined Arms 11.2 An attacking force with infantry, cavalry and artillery gains a combat odds shift of one column to the right. Counter-Battery Fire 13.5 A reduction in the strength of a bombarding artillery unit by half the strength of artillery in the target hex.  Die Rolls: Use one six-sided die (d6) for all die rolls. Division: Three units belonging to the same division may stack 3 units to the hex. Division I.D. is used in the Initial Set Up, and to specify regular and alternate reinforcements. 3.22, 3.23, 5.21, 6.1, 6.34 DRM (Die Roll Modifier): Number to be added to or subtracted from the die roll in specific situations. Engage 4.3 The act of entering an Enemy Zone of Control—this requires an attack upon the adjacent Enemy units.. Artillery when bombarding are not “Engaged.” 4.4, 10.31, 11.1, 16.26, 20.51 EZOC: Zone of Control (see p. 24) of an Enemy unit. Force: (a) a single stack, or (b) a Formation, excluding any out of command units. Any part of a formation in command now—or in the next Command Phase—can qualify as a force. Mode Card Play 1.5, 8.0, 12.36, 16.26, 19.32, 20.0, 24.55 Formation: A group of Combat Units with an Officer that shares the same Command Designation, linked by color. French formations are of corps size; Russian and British formations are divisions. 5.1, 5.23, 6,31, 6.4, 7.55, 11.3, 16.14, 17.41, 18.2, 19.2, 20.2, 20.42, 21.0, 22,23 Friendly: Friendly units are all those under the control of one player; enemy units are controlled by his opponent. Coalition Forces may include Prussian, Russian, Austrian, Swedish, British and other units.
