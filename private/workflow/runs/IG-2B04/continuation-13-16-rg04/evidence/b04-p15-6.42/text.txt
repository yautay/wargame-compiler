6.42 When a Phalanx unit enters two hexes of different terrain or
elevation, it expends the higher of the two possible costs both for
movement and cohesion. Whenever one half of the unit receives
Cohesion Hits, the entire unit suffers the effect. However, if one-
half of the Phalanx has already entered a hex that would cause
Cohesion Hits, moving the other half into the same type of hex
does not cause additional hits.
