DESIGN NOTE: This is a game mechanic that is intended to
simulate the effect of superiority in command. As such, it is
likely to be used almost exclusively by the player with palpably
better leaders (such as Hannibal and Scipio). The “inferior”
player will often find that, for him, its best application is when
it backfires on his supposedly better opponent. To that extent,
players should be aware that, simply because this option is
available, doesn’t mean it should be used indiscriminately.
