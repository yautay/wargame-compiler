6.24 Certain hexes are impassable: no unit may enter such a hex.
The Aufidus River and the town of Cannae, both on the Can-
nae map, are examples of such hexes. The Roman Camp on the
Beneventum map has its own Terrain Effects Chart. The dirt road
on the Zama map has no effect; the Roman Road at Beneventum
does have some slight effect on movement. Woods, Broken and
Marsh hexes are considered Rough Terrain.
