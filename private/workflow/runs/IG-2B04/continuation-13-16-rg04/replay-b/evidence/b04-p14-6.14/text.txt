6.14 Leaders (and Leader Elephants) may move any number
of times in a Game Turn, but they may not exceed their 9 MA
per Orders Phase. All other restrictions and allowances apply,
including the ability of leaders to use Orderly Withdrawal before
any opposing unit. A leader who has used his 9 MP in an Orders
Phase is not Finished; he simply may not move again that Orders
Phase, although he can still withdraw (4.72).
