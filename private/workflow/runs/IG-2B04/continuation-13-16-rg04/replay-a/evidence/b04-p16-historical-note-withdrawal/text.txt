HISTORICAL NOTE: Orderly Withdrawal is a “retreat’ in
the face of an approaching enemy, long before that enemy
can close. Therefore, the withdrawing unit can return to the
original facing (with minimum cohesion cost) it had before
the enemy approached. In play/game terms, you simply move
the counter backwards. In addition, the manipular tactics
and training of the legions enabled them to undertake this
maneuver with somewhat greater ease.
