5.51 A player with an Elite Overall Commander (indicated by
the triangle [▲] next to his Initiative Rating) has the option to
start each Game Turn with a single Elite Initiative Orders (EIO)
Phase. This EIO Phase may be undertaken either by the Overall
Commander, or any other friendly leader, regardless of Initiative
Rating, as designated by the player. For that leader to use the
EIO Phase, he must be within the OC’s Command Range. On
the first Game Turn, the player with the Elite Commander may
give his EIO Phase to any friendly leader, regardless of location.
