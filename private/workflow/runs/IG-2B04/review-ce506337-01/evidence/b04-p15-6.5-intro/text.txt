Orderly Withdrawal (OW) is a form of retreat—reaction move-
ment as it were—carried out during the enemy Movement and
Missile Fire Segment in which faster and more maneuverable
units can avoid getting entangled in Shock Combat. The in-
creased professionalism and training of armies of this era gave
them somewhat of an advantage in undertaking this maneuver
vis-à-vis the armies of the Hoplite Era.
