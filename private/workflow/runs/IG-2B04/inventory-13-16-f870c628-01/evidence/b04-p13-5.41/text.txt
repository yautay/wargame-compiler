5.41 Active Player Trump Option: The player whose turn it
normally would be to activate his lowest-rated leader may, in-
stead, attempt to activate any other inactive friendly leader with
a higher Initiative rating. To do this, the player must roll the lat-
ter’s Initiative rating or less on the die. If successful that leader
is now activated; if unsuccessful, the originally designated leader
is activated and the one who attempted the Trump is Finished.
