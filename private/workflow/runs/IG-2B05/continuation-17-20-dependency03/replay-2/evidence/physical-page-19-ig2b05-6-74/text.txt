6.74 Units In Column move into either their front hex or the
flank hexes adjacent to the front hex. To enter a front-flank hex,
a one-hex unit is first faced toward the front-flank hex and then
moved into the hex. For Phalanx units, the counter is turned
while moving so that the front half of the counter ends up in the
front-flank hex while the rear of the counter occupies the hex
formerly occupied by the front half of the counter. There is no
MP cost or cohesion penalty for making these facing changes.
Non-Phalanx units wishing to move into any other flank or rear
hex pay normal MP costs to change facing. Phalanx units cannot
change facing in this manner.
