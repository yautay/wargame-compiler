6.66 If two units are stacked in the same hex, the stacking order
(i.e., who is on top) may be changed/switched only by the change
stacking Individual Order (5.22 #6); it may not be part of a move
order or a Line Command. If the player wants to unstack the
units, only the top unit may move in that Orders Phase (9.66 is
an exception). A unit moving into a hex to stack is always placed
on the bottom. Such a move to a hex adjacent to an enemy unit
