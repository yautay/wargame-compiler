EXAMPLE OF WITHDRAWAL: [Cannae] A Roman Cavalry unit
(MA 8) is in 3214, facing northwest. A Roman Velites (MA 6) is in
3216, facing southwest. An African Phalanx is in 2912/3012. The
Carthaginian player moves the Phalanx towards the cavalry. As
the Phalanx enters 3112/3013 (having expended 1 of its 5 MA),
the Roman player withdraws the cavalry 2 hexes (he could have
withdrawn up to 3 hexes) to 3415. The cavalry does not take a
TQ check, and suffers no automatic cohesion hits, as it was not
approached from a flank or rear hex. The Phalanx now moves 1
hex to 3113/3213, and again into 3114/3214, placing it within 2
hexes of both the cavalry and the Velites. The cavalry withdraws
again, this time 1 hex into 3515. The Velites can withdraw 1 MP
(6–5=1) and moves to 3316, maintaining the same southwest
facing, incurring 1 Cohesion Hit since it was approached from
the flank. The Phalanx, with 2 MP left, continues to move into
3215/3314, where it is again within 2 hexes of both units, which
retreat to 3616 and 3417, respectively, with the Velites incurring
another Cohesion Hit in the process. The Phalanx spends its final
MP to move into 3315/3415. Although it is again within 2 hexes
of both Roman units, the Roman player chooses not to withdraw,
since the Phalanx has no MP remaining.
