DESIGN NOTE: On the battlefield, formed Infantry, which here
includes anything from PHs down to LIs, moved in a battle
formation that would, in later centuries, be called Line. They
were capable, however, of using a loose, more mobile forma-
tion (in modern terms, Column) for more rapid movement. In
addition, because the units were not in an extended line that
constantly needed dressing to be effective, the effects of move-
ment upon a unit’s cohesion were not as drastic.
