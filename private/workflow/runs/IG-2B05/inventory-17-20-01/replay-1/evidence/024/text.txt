EXAMPLE #2 (Rout/Retreat)
Continuing the example above, should the XIV Velites be routed
instead, all stationary units other than Skirmishers or Velites
would incur 1 Hit. If forced to stop in a friendly-occupied hex,
the Velites would move one more hex. For stationary units other
than Skirmishers/Velites, the player would apply 1 Hit, then roll
a die and apply Hits equal to the die roll minus the stationary
unit’s TQ, if the DR was greater. For our routing Legion XIV
Hastati, moving through any HA/PR/CO/TR (not stacked) from
the XIV Legion/Ala would be without incident. Any other unit or
stack would cost the stationary unit(s) 1 Hit. However, stopping in
any occupied hex other than one with a Velites of the same color,
forces a TQ check and a Cohesion Hit for the stationary unit.
