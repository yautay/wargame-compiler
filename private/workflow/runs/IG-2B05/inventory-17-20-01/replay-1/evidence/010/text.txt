6.61 The Basic, non-Roman Stacking Rule: Only one combat
unit (or one half of a Phalanx unit) may occupy a hex at the end
of any form of movement—voluntary or involuntary. Leaders
(including leader elephants) and informational markers do not
count for stacking purposes. A hex may contain any number of
leaders/markers. There are two exceptions to this basic rule: Ro-
man Manipular flexibility reflected in the stacking charts (6.63,
6.69) and Double-depth Phalanxes (9.51).
