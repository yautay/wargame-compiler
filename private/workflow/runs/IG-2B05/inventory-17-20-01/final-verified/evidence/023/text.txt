A Roman Hastati of Legion XIV can move through any infantry
unit of the XIV Legion/Ala without penalty. However, the Hastati
would incur (along with the stationary unit) 1 Hit when moving
through any other unit. In addition, the Hastati would incur 1
Hit when moving through a stack of HA/PR/CO/TR units of the
XIV Legion/Ala. The XIV Hastati could stop in a hex with any
other HA/PR/CO/TR unit, but all units in the stack would incur
a +1 DRM to all their TQ checks.
