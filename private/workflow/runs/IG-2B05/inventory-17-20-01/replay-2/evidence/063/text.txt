6.82 The “P” in PW means just that: the tactic must be designated
at the start of the Game Turn, before any leader activates. To
do so, the player places a PRE-ARRANGED WITHDRAWAL
marker under the leader that will enable the tactic. Now, let’s
face it, doing this will alert the opponent that something is up,
so the player is always free to place the marker with the NO
PW side of the marker face up to disguise his intent. More than
one marker may be placed if there is more than one Line with a
leader meeting the requirements of 6.81 above.
