6.76 If an In Column unit is Shock attacked, the unit automati-
cally goes back to its non-column formation (i.e., remove the In
Column marker) at the conclusion of that Orders Phase at the cost
of 1 Cohesion Hit. Phalanx units do not reface; all other units
must be adjusted 30 degrees to the left or right (owning player’s
choice) so that it faces a vertex.
