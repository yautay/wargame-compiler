EXAMPLE #1: [Cannae] A Phalanx unit in 3722/3822 has three
Velites units in each of its three frontal hexes (3622/3721/3821).
It must attack all defending units (which total their Size) as one
unit. If a Celtic Infantry unit was in 3920 and capable of attacking
the Roman unit in 3821, the Phalanx would have to engage only
the other two Velites. Or, unless the Celts had a Shock marker,
they could sit this one out while the phalangites took care of
everyone. While the former will produce better results, the latter
may be necessary if the Celts are close to their TQ limit in Hits.
