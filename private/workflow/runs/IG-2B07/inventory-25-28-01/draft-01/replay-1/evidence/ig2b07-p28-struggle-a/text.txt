The Hand-to-Hand Struggle: Starting with Phalanx A, the play-
ers consult the Clash of Spears and Swords Chart to determine
the base column to be used on the Shock Combat Results Table.
Phalanx A’s attack is frontal but since there is more than one
Type of defender, the Roman player gets to choose which to use.
Of the two Types the LG is more advantageous to him with a
base column of 7 and in looking at the Shock Superiority Chart,
there is no advantage to either side. Moreover, since the attack
is frontal, there is no Position Superiority. The players next
determine the effect of the Size ratio. Phalanx A with Size 10 is
exactly offset by the sum of the three Roman Sizes (4+3+3). There
are no shifts, so Phalanx A’s attack will be resolved on column
7. The Carthaginian player rolls a 6 which gives a “2(2)” result.
Phalanx A incurs 2 Hits. The Carthaginian player decides to use
the Phalanx special rule to allocate the Hits and gives both to
Cohort V (now 4 Hits).
