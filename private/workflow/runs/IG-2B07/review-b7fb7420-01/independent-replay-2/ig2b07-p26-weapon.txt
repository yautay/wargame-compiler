B. Weapon System Superiority: If no Position Superiority is
attained, the players check the opposing units’ weapon class re-
lationship (see the Shock Superiority Chart, 8.72) to determine if
either Weapon System is superior (either AS or DS) to the other.
If more than one unit on either side is involved in the combat,
the units chosen in 8.45 are used in the determination.
