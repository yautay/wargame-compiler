8.43 The Charge: All units with a SHOCK MUST CHECK TQ
marker and their intended targets undergo a Pre-Shock TQ check,
with the players rolling a die for each unit (all such checks being
simultaneous). Attacking units with SHOCK NO TQ CHECK
markers—and their defenders—do not undergo this TQ check.
If a unit is attacked by both types of units (Shock Check and
Shock No Check), the defender then checks TQ.
