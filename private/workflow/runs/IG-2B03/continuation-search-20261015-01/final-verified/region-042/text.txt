4.74 All leaders, other than the OC, may be replaced when
eliminated. This is done by having the OC issue an IO to replace
a fallen subordinate with a Replacement leader. Eliminated
Tribunes and Praefects are simply placed back on the map. The
OC does not have to be within range of any unit; simply place
the Replacement leader (or returning Tribune/Praefect) in any
hex containing a unit the leader can command. The Replacement
leader is Finished for that Game Turn. The number of Replace-
ment leaders provided is a design-intent play limitation. If there
are no Replacement leaders available, a leader may not be
replaced. This, again, does not apply to Tribunes and Praefects.
