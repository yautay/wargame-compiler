4.54 Praefects Equitum may issue Individual Orders to either
Legion or Ala cavalry, but only to one of these groups in a single
Orders Phase. However, they may issue a Line Command to a
line of mixed Legion and Ala cavalry if it is specifically allowed
by the Line Command Eligibility Chart (e.g., it is not allowed at
Cannae). The Legion/Ala restrictions do not apply for purposes
of 7.33. A Praefect Equitum may enable Shock for any Roman
cavalry unit.
