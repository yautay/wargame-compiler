EXAMPLE OF ORDER OF ACTIVATION: At the start of the first
turn of Bagradas Plains, all leaders are available for activation.
After the two Praefects Sociorum have been activated and have
finished giving orders, the Tribunes/Praefects Equitum will be
activated next, followed by Bostar and Hamilcar. When it comes
to who is next, both Regulus and Hasdrubal have 5 Initiative
Ratings, so there would be a die roll to see who goes next.
