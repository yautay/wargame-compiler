4.71 Leaders may enter an enemy ZOC only if stacked with a
friendly combat unit (and Leader Elephants are combat units) or
if that ZOC is already occupied by a friendly combat unit. Like
combat units, leaders too must cease movement upon entering an
enemy ZOC. A leader (including his Leader Elephant) may leave
an enemy ZOC only if he receives order(s) from his OC (4.4).
