One of Hannibal’s clearest areas of superiority was his command
system. Through extensive briefings and campaigns, Hannibal’s
seconds-in-command were fully capable of acting on their own
to carry out Hannibal’s plans. Therefore, all Carthaginian Sub-
ordinate Commanders, in battles where Hannibal is the Overall
Commander— except where indicated in the scenario—can issue
Line Commands during their Initial Orders Phases without being
within Hannibal’s range and/or resorting to a Strategy Rating DR.
During Momentum-generated Phases, 4.33 applies.
