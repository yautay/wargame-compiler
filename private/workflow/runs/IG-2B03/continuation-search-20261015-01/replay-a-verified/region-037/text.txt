HISTORICAL DIGRESSION: It seems that to work for Car-
thage you either had to be Hannibal’s brother or be named
Hasdrubal or Hanno. The number of “different” command-
ers with these last two names is frightening. From a design
point of view, trying to figure out which one was where is
even worse. Then there are the “Car Boys”: Hamilcar and
Bomilcar. For sanity’s sake, we have grouped all the Hannos
and Hasdrubals into one of each. Their ratings were pretty
much the same anyway.
