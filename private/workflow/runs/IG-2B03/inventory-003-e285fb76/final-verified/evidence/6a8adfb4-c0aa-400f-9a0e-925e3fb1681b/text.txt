4.53 Tribunes and Praefects Sociorum may issue a Line Com-
mand to any one line of infantry listed on the Line Command
Eligibility Chart for that battle. For example, at Zama, a Tribune
can issue an LC for all Hastati plus any additional Ala Cohorts;
a Praefect Sociorum could issue a LC to a line of Ala Cohorts
and Triarii; etc.
