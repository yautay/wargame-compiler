2.21 Combat units come in two sizes: square and rectangular, the
latter representing the rigid, Macedonian-style Phalanx formation
still used by some heavy infantry. Each combat unit is a specific
type (as illustrated below), depending on its weaponry and body
armor/protection. All combat units are rated numerically for
their Size, Troop Quality (TQ), and Movement capability. Units
that are capable of firing missiles also have a Missile Indicator,
indicating the class of missiles used (A = arrow, J = javelin and S
= stone). The Missile Table gives the Ranges available for these
missiles. Each combat unit has two sides. When a unit is flipped
to its reverse side, that side indicates that it is Depleted (10.3).
