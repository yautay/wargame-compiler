Note to Players: SPQR, while not an overly difficult game to
learn, understand, or play, uses a tactical system which most re-
wards those who know what their men can do and how to exploit
the capabilities of even the worst of them. As warfare evolved
from the previous Alexandrian era and became infinitely more
complex, the rules reflect, albeit only slightly, that increased
complexity. To calmly blunder into battle with a “let’s see what
happens here” theory is to be exiled quickly to some forlorn rock
in the Mediterranean.
