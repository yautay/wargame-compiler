8.51 Attacking units and any leaders stacked with them must
advance into any hex vacated by enemy units because of Shock
Combat (8.43, 8.47), with the exception that an attacker in the
ZOC of another enemy unit does not advance in the Charge
step (8.43). Attacking units stacked together advance together,
maintaining their stacking order. Advancing after combat does
not require an order, but the advancing unit incurs all cohesion
costs engendered by such a move. If the advance would rout
(10.2) the moving unit, it advances and does not rout; the number
of Hits it has is set equal to TQ minus 1.
