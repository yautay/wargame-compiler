8.1 MISSILE FIRE
HISTORICAL NOTE: Missile units, with their lower TQs and
susceptibility to shock by heavier units, appear to be “weak”
units. However, they have two abilities which make them quite
useful: they can (usually) fire during movement and most can
use Harassment & Dispersal fire. Their combat effective-
ness was limited, though, because of a lack of penetrative
capability—a problem that would remain until the inventions
of the crossbow and the Welsh longbow.
