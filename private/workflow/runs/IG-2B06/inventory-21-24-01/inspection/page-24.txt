PLAY NOTE: The Shock system relies on the interaction of the
units’ weapons, armor protection, size, angle of attack, and
troop quality (TQ), to produce a single result. While no factor
is unimportant, the rating to pay closest attention to is the TQ,
for that rating determines the ability of a unit to withstand the
rigors of toe-to-toe ancient warfare.
PROCEDURE
Shock Combat takes place in the Shock Combat Segment. Shock
is part of a leader’s Orders Phase, and all Shock precipitated by
that leader is resolved before the next leader may be activated
or Momentum attempted.
