EXAMPLE: A Macedonian Phalanx moving adjacent to a Roman
Hastati unit has a SHOCK MUST CHECK TQ marker placed on
top of it because it must attack (7.31). A Numidian Light Cavalry
unit moving adjacent to a Principes unit does not have to Shock
attack; however, the moving player must state his decision on
whether to do so as it finishes movement. If he wishes it to attack,
he places a SHOCK MUST CHECK TQ marker on top of the unit.
