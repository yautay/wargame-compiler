7.31 In the Shock Combat Segment, all friendly “heavy” units—
those whose Type is marked with a plus sign (+) on the Shock
Superiority Chart—that moved adjacent to an enemy unit from
a non-adjacent hex during the preceding Movement and Missile
Fire Segment must Shock attack all enemy units in their ZOC. All
other “light” Shock-capable units—no plus sign (+)—that moved
adjacent to an enemy unit from a non-adjacent hex during the
preceding Movement and Missile Fire Segment may choose to
Shock attack all enemy units in their ZOC in the ensuing Shock
Combat Segment.
