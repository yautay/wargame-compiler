There are two kinds of Combat: Missile and Shock. Missile
Combat occurs as part of—or instead of—movement at any point
during the Movement and Missile Fire Segment of an Orders
Phase. Shock Combat comes in its own segment which occurs
after all orders have been issued and movement is completed. A
missile unit can participate in both types of combat during the
same Orders Phase. In Shock Combat, the Type, Size, and Troop
Quality of a unit are considered. The effects of combat are Cohe-
sion Hits, which may result in a unit being Routed.
