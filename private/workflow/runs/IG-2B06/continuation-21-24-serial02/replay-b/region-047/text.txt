8.15 Missile Fire Resolution: The Missile Range and Results
Chart (MRRC) is used to determine the effect of Missile Fire.
Each missile unit has a missile strength which varies with the
range, as given on the chart. For each Missile Fire, a die is rolled.
If the DR is equal to or less than the strength of the missile unit at
that range, then the target unit receives 1 Cohesion Hit (2 Cohe-
sion Hits in certain cases). Adjustments to the DR are listed on
the MRRC. All DRMs are cumulative unless noted otherwise on
