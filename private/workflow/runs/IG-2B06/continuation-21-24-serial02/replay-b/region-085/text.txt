8.31 Missile-armed Skirmishers (SK) and Light Infantry (LI)
may use Harassment & Dispersal fire (H&D) against any enemy
unit that has the same or lower Movement Allowance (MA).
Missile-armed Light Cavalry (LC) may use H&D against any
enemy unit that has a lower MA. H&D fire requires a move
order, is movement for purposes of Missile Fire modifiers, and
consumes the unit’s entire MA.
