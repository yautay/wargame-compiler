The Macedon LC D has no target in range for regular Missile
Fire, but all Roman units are within 4 hex range for H&D fire.
However, D’s LOS and path to Roman unit W is blocked by two
friendly units. D does have a clear LOS and path to Roman
units X, Y, and Z (orange arrows). D’s Missile Fire against any
of the targets would be at range 1, with a +1 DRM since the
Macedon LC is mounted and is considered moving for H&D
fire. Targeting Z is advantageous because D can trace a path
such that only the target unit is able to Reaction Fire. An attack
on X or Y would draw an additional Reaction Fire from Y or
Z. Whichever Roman unit is chosen as the target may use Entry
Reaction Fire in response.
