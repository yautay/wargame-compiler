Thrace Peltast C has no target in range for regular Missile Fire
but can use H&D fire from a range of 2 hexes (black arrow). C
does not have a LOS to Roman unit X since the LOS goes down
a hexside between blocking/non-blocking hexes. C does have
a LOS and clear path to Roman unit Y so can use H&D fire
resolved at range 1. Note the javelinists are not penalized for
moving. Both X and Y may use Entry Reaction Fire at range 1
against the H&D fire, but the fire is considered simultaneous as
opposed to being resolved first.
