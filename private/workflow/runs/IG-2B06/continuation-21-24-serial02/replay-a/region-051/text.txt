EXAMPLE: [Cannae] Balearic slingers are 2 hexes distant from
a Roman cavalry unit. At a range of 2 hexes, the slingers’ Missile
Strength is 2. The Carthaginian player fires at the Roman unit,
rolling a 2. The Roman cavalry incurs 1 Cohesion Hit. If the
slingers had moved to get within range, the +1 DRM for moving
would have caused the fire to be ineffective. If the player had
rolled, say, a 6, the fire would have had no effect. If he had rolled
a 9, it would have had no effect AND he would have had to place
a MISSILE LOW marker (8.17) on the slingers.
