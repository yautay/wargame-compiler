8.32 To use H&D, Light Cavalry (LC) must be within 4 hexes,
and infantry types within 2 hexes of, but not adjacent to, the
target, and cannot be in an enemy ZOC. The unit must have a
LOS to the target and be able to trace a path of hexes through
its Front hexes (only) clear of any combat units and enemy
ZOC unless that ZOC is adjacent to the target. The path may
not be traced through an impassable hex, Rough terrain hex, or
up/down a hexside of more than 1 level of elevation change. It
may cross a Stream.
