Description

We are proud to bring SPQR back in print in a new and expanded 5th Edition. This edition will not only include the scenarios from the Barbarian module but also the six scenarios of The Hammer of God module published in C3i magazine and an updated version of the Battle of Pydna originally published in the SPQR Player’s Guide. Perhaps the most significant change to the original SPQR and Scenario Pack scenarios is the inclusion of the setups to play with the Simple GBoH 2nd Edition rules!

The game will feature a new edition of the rulebook incorporating all known errata and numerous clarifications based on player feedback. Known errata on the Player Aid cards have been addressed. The Game Turn Track Display will be replaced by a Rout Point Tracking card (which includes a Game Turn Track) in the same style as that in Great Battles of Julius Caesar. There are no changes to the maps. Minor errata on a few of the counters will be addressed and the counters associated with the early C3i Marathon scenario will be replaced with counters for the Pydna scenario along with additional counters to eliminate several counter substitutions.

Due to the increased content, the most significant change to the components is a restructuring of the previous edition’s three scenario/module books into three scenario books arranged chronologically.

The Rise of Rome (315 BCE through 225 BCE)
Lautulae, 315 BCE (Barbarian)
Tifernum, 297 BCE (Barbarian)
Sentinum, 295 BCE (Barbarian)
Heraclea, 280 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Ausculum, 277 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Beneventum, 275 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Bagradas Plains, 255 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Telamon, 225 BCE (Barbarian)

The 2nd Punic War (218 BCE -200 BCE)
Trebbia, 218 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Cannae, 216 BCE (SPQR Deluxe) 
Baecula, 208 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Metaurus, 207 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Ilipa, 206 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Great Plains, 204 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Zama, 202 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Cremona, 200 BCE (Barbarian)

Rome and the Successors (197 BCE - 109 BCE)
Cynoscephalae, 197 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Magnesia, 190 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)
Pydna, 168 BCE (C3i SPQR Player’s Guide)
Nahal El-Haramiah, 166 BCE (C3i Hammer of God)
Battle Of Beth Horon, 165 BCE (C3i Hammer of God)
Battle Of Emmaus, 165 BCE (C3i Hammer of God)
Battle Of Beth Zur, 164 BCE (C3i Hammer of God)
Battle Of Beth-Zechariah, 162 BCE (C3i Hammer of God)
Battle Of Elasa, 162 BCE (C3i Hammer of God)
Muthul River, 109 BCE (SPQR Deluxe)

GMT will offer an update kit which will include the rulebook, scenario books, Player Aid Cards and Display, and a sheet of 60 counters for use with the new scenario, counter corrections, and additional counters to eliminate several counter substitutions.

Components:
  • One Rulebook
  • Three Chronological Scenario Books
  • Four 11” x 17” Player Aid Cards
  • One 8 ½” x 11” Rout Point Tracking/Game Turn Card
  • Six 22” x 34” Paper Maps (two-sided)
  • Five and a half Countersheets
  • One 10-sided Die


SPQR Game Designers: Richard Berg and Mark Herman
Developer: Alan J. Ray
Barbarian Module and Pydna Scenarios: Dan Fournie
