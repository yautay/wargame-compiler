4.32 Line Composition: In a reflection of training and discipline
levels, as well as tactical usage, the units in most lines must be
adjacent. Any space between units ends the line. In certain other
lines, each unit must be within 2 (and occasionally 3) hexes of at
least one other unit in the line. If there is an intervening space,
it may not be occupied by an enemy unit, a friendly unit from
a different line (Skirmishers and Velites are an exception, see
9.2), or an enemy ZOC. The units which may use a two-space
(or three-space) Line are noted on the scenario’s Line Command
Eligibility Chart.
