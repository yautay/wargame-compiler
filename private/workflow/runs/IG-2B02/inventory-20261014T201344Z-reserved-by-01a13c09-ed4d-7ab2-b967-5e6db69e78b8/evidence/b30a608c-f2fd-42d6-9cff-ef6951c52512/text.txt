4.24 A leader, other than the Overall Commander (OC), who is
in an enemy ZOC may not issue orders either to combat units
or to himself, and thus may not move out of that enemy ZOC
unless and until he is moved out of that ZOC by an IO from the
OC (4.4). While in an enemy ZOC, the only IO an OC may issue
is to move himself. See also 5.26.
