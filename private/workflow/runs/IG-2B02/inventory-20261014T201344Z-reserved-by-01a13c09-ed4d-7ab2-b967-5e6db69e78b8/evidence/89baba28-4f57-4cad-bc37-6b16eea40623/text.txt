A Line can be of any length provided that the units meet the ad-
jacency requirements and all the units in the Line have the same
orientation—either Flank-to-Flank or (but not and) Front-to-Rear
(see 7.1). For units In Column, treat the Flank hexes adjacent to
the Rear hex as Rear hexes. If the units meet the adjacency re-
quirements, but do not have the same orientation, only those units
within the leader’s Command Range can be included in the Line.
