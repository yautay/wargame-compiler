# wgc-review

Read docs/work/WORKFLOW.md, the selected card, packet and frozen delivery manifest.
Use a reviewer identity distinct from the executor. Verify hashes before assessing work.
Check contracts, failures, completeness and provenance of expected results. For legal
actions, agreement with the validator alone cannot prove absence of omitted choices.
For meaning, inspect exactly the assigned source evidence and record observations by
revision/aspect. A model cannot create the owner's semantic acceptance.
Write findings with location, consequence, evidence and required verification; do not
silently edit the implementation. After repairs review changed hashes and affected risks.
Coordinator records acceptance/done only after technical and required human gates pass.
