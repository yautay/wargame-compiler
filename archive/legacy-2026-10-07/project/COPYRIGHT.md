# Copyright and Third-Party Content Policy

Wargame Compiler is a tool for analyzing, modeling, digitizing, translating, and publishing board wargame rules and
related materials. This repository's license, when added, applies to the project's own code, schemas, documentation,
synthetic examples, and other original project materials. It does not grant rights to third-party game content.

This document is project guidance, not legal advice.

## User-supplied materials

Users are responsible for making sure they have the rights or permission required for the materials they provide to
the tool. Depending on the material and intended use, that may include rights to read, analyze, translate, digitize,
reproduce, or distribute the input.

The project does not give users permission to use someone else's rulebooks, maps, counter sheets, artwork, cards,
scenario books, VASSAL modules, trademarks, or other protected materials.

## Repository content policy

The public tool repository should primarily contain:

- original project code;
- schemas and data contracts;
- synthetic examples and self-authored benchmark materials;
- original benchmark data;
- public-domain materials;
- materials used with permission.

Source text for commercial games should normally stay outside this repository. Inventories, hashes, anchors, and
short control quotes may be used where appropriate to make local processing reproducible without redistributing the
source material.

## Commercial game content

Do not publicly commit or redistribute commercial game materials unless the repository has the rights or permission
to do so. This includes, but is not limited to:

- full rulebooks;
- scans or extracted text of protected rulebooks;
- maps;
- counter sheets;
- artwork;
- scenarios;
- cards;
- VASSAL modules or other files that function as unauthorized copies of protected game materials;
- other protected assets from publishers, designers, artists, or licensors.

Descriptive references to games, publishers, systems, or formats may remain in documentation when they are needed to
explain compatibility, tests, research context, interoperability, provenance, or project history.

## Generated outputs

Generated outputs may still include or derive from protected third-party material. Creating a translation, digital
module, LaTeX/PDF output, player aid, extracted table, helper asset, or engine package does not automatically mean the
output may be shared publicly.

Before distributing generated outputs, users should confirm that the source materials, transformations, and intended
distribution are permitted.

Game titles, publisher names, series names, system names, logos, product names, and tool names may be protected by
trademark or other rights. This project may use names such as publishers, games, systems, or VASSAL descriptively for
identification, documentation, testing, interoperability, or research.

Such use should not imply affiliation, sponsorship, approval, or endorsement unless that relationship is explicitly
documented.

## Takedown and rights concerns

If a rights owner or authorized representative believes that this repository contains material that should not be
published here, they can raise the concern through the repository's normal GitHub channels, such as an issue or pull
request. Please identify the material, the rights concern, and the requested action so maintainers can review it.
